Site plan: Benchtop Repair

This plan covers the name, purpose, audience, colors, fonts and layout for my computer repair website.

Site name

Benchtop Repair

A technician fixes computers on a workbench, so the name tells visitors that real hands-on repair happens here. It is short, easy to remember and easy to spell.

Optional domain: benchtoprepair.com

Site purpose

The site is the online front desk for a local computer repair shop. It lists repair services with starting prices and turnaround times, helps visitors guess what is wrong with a symptom helper, and lets them send a repair request and see their saved requests.

The pages are Home, Services, Book a repair and References.

Scenarios

Questions that a visitor from the target audience will ask. The target audience is students, families and small business owners who rely on a computer every day and are not technical.

  • My laptop screen is cracked. How much will it cost and how long will it take?
  • My computer is very slow. Is it worth fixing, or should I buy a new one?
  • Can I get my photos back from a drive that stopped working?
  • When is the shop open, and how do I book a drop-off time?

Color schema

The colors come from a repair bench: dark workshop ink, the green of a circuit board and the amber of a status light.

Typography

Bricolage Grotesque

Used for the site name and all headings. It has a confident, slightly mechanical look that suits a workshop.

Source Sans 3

Used for body text, navigation, buttons and forms. It stays clear and readable at small sizes on phones. Both fonts fall back to the system sans-serif font if they cannot load.

Wireframes

Home page layout. On mobile everything stacks in one column and the menu collapses. On wider screens the hero splits into text and image, and the steps and cards sit in rows.
