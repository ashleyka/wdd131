Bulawayo, Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe is a landlocked country in southern Africa. Bulawayo, my home city, is the country's second largest and is known as the "City of Kings and Queens." Zimbabwe is home to Victoria Falls, one of the largest waterfalls in the world, and to Great Zimbabwe, the stone ruins of a medieval city that gave the country its name.
