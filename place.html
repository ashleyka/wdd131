About Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe is a landlocked country in southern Africa, bordered by South Africa, Botswana, Zambia, and Mozambique. It is home to Victoria Falls, one of the Seven Natural Wonders of the World, and to the stone ruins of Great Zimbabwe, a medieval city that gave the country its name. From the granite hills of Bulawayo to the highlands around Harare, Zimbabwe's landscapes and wildlife make it one of southern Africa's most rewarding places to visit.
